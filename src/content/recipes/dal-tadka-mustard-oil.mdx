Arhar dal is the most ordinary dish in an Indian kitchen, and that is exactly why the oil matters. A kachi ghani mustard tadka gives it the sharp, warm finish most of us grew up with.

## You need

- 1 cup arhar (toor) dal, washed
- 1 tomato, chopped
- ½ tsp haldi, salt to taste
- 2 tbsp wood-pressed yellow mustard oil
- 1 tsp jeera, a pinch of hing, 2 dried red chillies, 4 cloves garlic

## Method

1. Pressure cook the dal with haldi, salt, tomato and 3 cups water for 3–4 whistles. Mash lightly.
2. Heat the mustard oil until it just starts to smoke. Turn off the flame for 20 seconds so it cools a little.
3. Turn the flame back on low. Add jeera, hing, red chillies and garlic. Let the garlic turn golden.
4. Pour the tadka over the dal and cover for a minute. Serve with rice or roti.

**Tip:** Smoking the oil first mellows the raw pungency of kachi ghani mustard oil.
