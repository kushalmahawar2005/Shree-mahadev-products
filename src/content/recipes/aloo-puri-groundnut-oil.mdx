Groundnut oil has a high smoke point and a gentle nutty taste, which is why it has always been the oil for puri, pakode and namkeen in Rajasthani homes.

## You need

- 2 cups atta, ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp oil for the dough
- Water as needed
- Wood-pressed groundnut oil for frying

## Method

1. Knead a stiff dough with atta, salt, 1 tsp oil and water. Rest for 15 minutes.
2. Roll small, even puris. Keep them covered so they do not dry out.
3. Heat the oil in a kadhai. Drop a small piece of dough in: it should rise immediately.
4. Fry one puri at a time, pressing gently with the slotted spoon so it puffs. Flip once.
5. Drain and serve hot with aloo sabzi.

**Tip:** Strain and store the oil after frying. Do not mix used oil with fresh oil in the bottle.
