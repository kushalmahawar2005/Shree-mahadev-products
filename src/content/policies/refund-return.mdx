## When we offer a replacement or refund

A replacement or refund is given **only** in these two cases:

- The product arrived **damaged or leaking**.
- The product is **expired** (past its expiry or shelf life) when it reaches you.

We do not offer returns or refunds for any other reason.

## Unboxing video is required

Please **record a video while opening your parcel**. A damage or expiry claim is accepted **only with this video**.

- Start recording **before** you open the parcel, with the shipping label and the sealed box clearly visible.
- Record in **one continuous video**, with no cuts or pauses, until the product is fully out.
- Clearly show the damage or leak, or the **expiry date** printed on the label.

Without an unboxing video, we cannot approve a replacement or refund.

## How to raise a request

1. Within **7 days of delivery**, open the order in **My account → My orders** and tap **Request return**, or raise a request in **Help & support**.
2. Choose the reason (damaged / expired) and **upload your unboxing video**. You can add photos of the box and label too.
3. Our team reviews it and gets back to you during support hours (9am–6pm, Mon–Sat).

## What we don't accept

- Change of mind, taste preference, or ordering the wrong item or size.
- Opened or used products, unless the product was damaged or expired on arrival.
- Natural changes that are normal for unrefined oils and raw honey: slight cloudiness, settled sediment, thickening in winter, or honey crystallising. These are signs the product has not been refined or processed.
