We ship our wood-pressed oils, raw honey and natural sweeteners from our unit in Bijainagar, Rajasthan, through courier partners booked via NimbusPost.

## Where we deliver

- We deliver within India only.
- At checkout, enter your 6-digit pincode. We check live courier coverage for that pincode and show the shipping charge and an estimated delivery time before you pay.
- If no courier serves your pincode, the website will tell you, and you will not be able to place the order. Please message us on WhatsApp and we will try to help.
