This policy explains what personal information **Swadeshi Mahadev Products** ("we") collects when you use swadeshimahadevproducts.com, and how we use and protect it.

## What we collect

- **Order details:** your name, mobile number, email, delivery address and pincode, and what you buy.
- **Checkout progress:** if you start checkout, we save the contact details you type and the items in your cart, so we can help you finish the order (for example, a WhatsApp reminder if you leave items in your cart).
- **Payment:** online payments are handled by **PayU**. We do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details. We only receive the payment status and a transaction reference.
- **Technical data:** basic data such as your IP address and browser, which our servers and providers use to keep the site working and secure.

## How we use it

- To process, ship and support your order, including calling or messaging you about it.
- To confirm cash on delivery orders and to send order and delivery updates.
- To follow up on carts you left, at most a couple of times. You can ask us to stop.
- To prevent fraud and keep records required by law (for example tax records).

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Who we share it with

Only what is needed, with:

- **PayU:** to take online payments.
- **NimbusPost and its courier partners:** your name, phone and address, to deliver your order.
- **Our hosting and service providers:** to run the website and store data securely (for example our web host and Cloudinary for product images).
- Government authorities, when the law requires it.

## Cookies

We use a few essential cookies: to keep your cart, to remember your checkout, and to keep our admin area secure. If we add analytics or advertising cookies later, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
