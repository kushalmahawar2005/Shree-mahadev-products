We get this question a lot, so here is the honest answer.

## Fine sediment

Our oil is settled naturally and filtered through cloth, not through industrial filters. A thin layer of seed particles can collect at the bottom of the bottle. Give it a gentle shake or leave it, it is safe either way.

## Winter thickening

Some oils, especially coconut and groundnut, start to thicken or turn cloudy when the temperature drops. Place the bottle in a warm spot for a while and it will clear.

## Colour changes between batches

Seeds change from harvest to harvest, so colour can vary a little from batch to batch. We do not bleach the oil to make it uniform.
